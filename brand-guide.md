# Pure Research Peptide Lab Brand Guide

## Positioning

Pure Research Peptide Lab should feel controlled, technical, private, and laboratory-first. The brand should not feel like a wellness shop, supplement store, gym brand, anti-aging clinic, med spa, or performance-enhancement brand.

Primary positioning line:

**Research-use peptides for qualified laboratory and analytical research only.**

Required compliance posture:

- Research use only.
- Not for human use.
- Not a drug, food, dietary supplement, cosmetic, or medical product.
- No dosing, cycle, injection, treatment, diagnosis, cure, prevention, performance, anti-aging, weight-loss, recovery, bodybuilding, or wellness claims.
- 21+ access gate before browsing product details or requesting information.
- Product pages should require an acknowledgement before viewing or ordering.
- Certificates of Analysis and batch documentation should be treated as quality documentation, not as safety or efficacy claims.

## Typography

Use a modern, legible sans-serif. System stack is acceptable:

`Inter, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`

Headlines should be direct and technical. Avoid hype words like optimize, transform, unlock, elite, powerful, miracle, body, recovery, fat loss, muscle, anti-aging, or healing.

## Voice

Clear, restrained, and documentation-oriented.

Good:

- Research use only.
- Batch-level documentation available.
- Independent analytical testing.
- Controlled inquiry process.
- Qualified laboratory research.

Avoid:

- Human outcomes.
- User testimonials.
- Dosing instructions.
- Medical condition references.
- Before/after language.
- Fitness, longevity, or wellness promises.

## Homepage Structure

1. Age and research-only notice.
2. Hero with restrained lab language.
3. Quality documentation section.
4. Five-product catalog without benefit claims.
5. Compliance statement.
6. Inquiry form with required acknowledgement.

## Product Naming

Current materials:

- Retatrutide 10 mg
- SS-31 10 mg
- Mots-C 10 mg
- BPC 157 10 mg
- NAD+ 500 mg

Product names and amounts should be treated as catalog identifiers only. Do not pair them with suggested use, dosing, administration, benefits, transformations, protocols, testimonials, or outcome claims.

## Footer Disclaimer

Suggested footer language:

Pure Research Peptide Lab materials are offered for laboratory research use only. Products are not for human consumption, diagnostic use, therapeutic use, food use, cosmetic use, or resale to consumers. Purchasers must be 21+ and responsible for compliance with all applicable laws and institutional requirements.
